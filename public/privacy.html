Privacy Policy – Moo Partner

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Moo Partner helps milk sellers keep track of daily deliveries, customer payments and business expenses. This policy explains what data the app stores and how it is used.

Data we store

  • Your account: your name, phone number, and your PIN (stored only as a one-way encrypted hash; we cannot read it).
  • Your business records: the customers you add (name, phone, address, rate), milk deliveries, payments received and expenses you enter.

This data is saved on your phone and copied to our server so it is backed up and available if you change phones.

Microphone

If you use voice search, the app asks for microphone access. Speech is converted to text by your phone's speech recognition service only while you are searching. The app does not record or store audio.

How we use your data

Only to run the app for you. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone, except when required by law. Bills you choose to share (for example on WhatsApp) are sent by you through your phone's share menu.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all its data at any time in the app: Menu → Settings → Delete account. Deletion is permanent and removes everything from our server.
